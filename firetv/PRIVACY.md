# NuniFlix for Fire TV: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

NuniFlix is a remote for your own NuniFlix server, which you or a friend runs for a Plex or Jellyfin library.

**What the app stores on your Fire TV:** the server address you type in, the sign-in token that server gives back, and your chosen theme. Nothing else. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

**Where the app connects:**
- Only to the NuniFlix server address you enter. That server's owner holds what you do there, like your requests and watch history in Plex or Jellyfin. Their own privacy terms apply.
- In demo mode, to `test-streams.mux.dev` for a public sample video (Big Buck Bunny). No personal data is sent.

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts of its own. It doesn't sell or share data with anyone. Away from your home network, it only connects to servers over https.

Amazon may collect device and usage data under its own terms as part of running the Appstore and Fire TV.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/GitNuni/NuniFlix/issues
